Verification of the submission deck, the screencast and the demo driver against
the nine requirements from the organizers. Artifacts under examination: the deck
source `presentation/Презентация Madrigal Protocol Lab.html`, its light twin
`presentation/Презентация Madrigal Protocol Lab (светлая).html`, the committed
`presentation/slide.pdf` and `presentation/slide-light.pdf`, the PPTX builds
`presentation/slide.pptx` and `presentation/slide-light.pptx`, and
`presentation/defense_script.md`. Nothing was rebuilt from a template; the PDFs
are printed from the HTML and the PPTX is generated by our own code.

## Fonts

The deck asks for Tektur (headings, the wordmark), Montserrat (body) and
JetBrains Mono (code, handles, the byte line). The `.ttf` files ship under
`presentation/public/fonts/` and are inlined into the HTML, so a machine without
the families installed still shows the deck's own typefaces. The GUI uses the
same families through `src/ui/theme/fonts.py`.

## Demo driver

`presentation/record_demo.py` drives the real window through the demo scenario
and pauses between steps so the take reads as a demonstration. It adds nothing to
the product and imports only the public window entry points.
